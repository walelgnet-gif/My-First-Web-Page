How to Make Homemade Pizza

A simple recipe for a crispy, cheesy pizza you can make at home.

Ingredients

For the dough

  • 1 packet (2 1/4 tsp) active dry yeast
  • 1 cup warm water
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 1/2 to 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

For the toppings

  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Pepperoni slices
  • Fresh basil leaves

Instructions

Mix the yeast, sugar, and warm water and let it sit for 5 minutes until foamy, then add the flour, salt, and olive oil and knead for about 8 minutes until smooth. Cover the dough and let it rise for 1 hour while you preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C). Stretch the dough onto a baking tray, spread on the sauce, sprinkle with cheese, add the pepperoni, and bake for 12 to 15 minutes until the crust is golden. Top with fresh basil, let it cool for a couple of minutes, then slice and enjoy.
